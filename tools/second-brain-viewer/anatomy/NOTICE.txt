brain-mesh.json retains the five named anatomical parts, 7353 vertices and 14686
triangles. Textures, materials and normals were omitted. Coordinates were scaled
from meters to millimeters, centered vertically by subtracting 0.063822 meters,
and rounded to three decimal places. Y points up; posterior is negative Z.
The viewer computes normals and applies its own material styling.

To reproduce, download the GLB from the URL above, verify its SHA256, then run:
python3 tools/second-brain-viewer/prepare-anatomy.py brain.glb
An optional second argument selects the output JSON path.

This is an illustrative anatomical model, not a patient scan or clinical atlas.
