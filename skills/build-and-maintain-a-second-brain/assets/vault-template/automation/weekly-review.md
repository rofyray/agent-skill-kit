# Scheduled weekly loop review

Operate on the vault containing this prompt. Read `SECOND_BRAIN.md` first and follow its REVIEW procedure for the seven calendar days ending today.

1. Find the latest successful lint event for this maintenance window—normally the lint scheduled immediately before this review—and its report. Do not rely only on the calendar date. If none exists, stop and report that review is waiting for lint rather than writing a partial review.
2. Read the period's log entries and every page they reference.
3. Identify the three most important ideas added or revised.
4. Explain meaningful new connections between recent and older pages.
5. Identify emerging hubs, recurring interests, unresolved conflicts, gaps, and provisional work.
6. Recommend a short next-exploration queue with reasons.
7. Save a collision-safe timestamped report under `reports/reviews/` without overwriting an existing report. Include the lint maintenance-run identifier and append it to the review event in `log.md`.

Ground the review in vault evidence. Do not treat recommendations as facts or make unrelated wiki changes.
