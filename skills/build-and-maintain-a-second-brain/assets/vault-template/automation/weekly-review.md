# Scheduled weekly loop review

Operate on the vault containing this prompt. Read `SECOND_BRAIN.md` first and follow its REVIEW procedure for the seven calendar days ending today.

1. Read the period's log entries and every page they reference.
2. Identify the three most important ideas added or revised.
3. Explain meaningful new connections between recent and older pages.
4. Identify emerging hubs, recurring interests, unresolved conflicts, gaps, and provisional work.
5. Recommend a short next-exploration queue with reasons.
6. Save `reports/reviews/YYYY-MM-DD-weekly-review.md` and append a review event to `log.md`.

Ground the review in vault evidence. Do not treat recommendations as facts or make unrelated wiki changes.
