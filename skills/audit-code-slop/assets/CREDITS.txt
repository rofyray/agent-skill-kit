Conceptual inspiration:
Sebastian, "If coding is solved, what now?: Measuring the sloppiness of code", Earendil.
https://earendil.com/posts/measuring-code-sloppiness/
The article motivated the paired verbosity and erosion measurements and their
interpretation. This skill's instructions and adapter are independently written.

Research and executable dependency:
Gabriel Orlanski and collaborators, SlopCodeBench.
https://github.com/SprocketLab/slop-code-bench
Gabriel Orlanski, scb-check, pinned dependency version 0.2.0.
https://github.com/gabeorlanski/scb-check
The adapter invokes installed parser, complexity, clone, and bundled-rule APIs.
Upstream code and rules are not vendored in this skill. Their packages retain
their own licenses. No endorsement by the cited authors is implied.

Multilingual parser dependency:
tree-sitter-language-pack contributors, pinned dependency version 0.10.0.
https://pypi.org/project/tree-sitter-language-pack/0.10.0/
https://github.com/xberg-io/tree-sitter-language-pack
Individual grammar authors are credited and licensed in that distribution.
This skill imports installed parsers. It does not vendor grammar code. The
multilingual rule logic, clone matcher, and measurement adapters are independently
authored and do not imply equivalent rule coverage to the Python research profile.
