The upstream project supplied valuable foundations including triage-before-
template coaching, the five-dimension interview rubric, persistent candidate
state, storybank and earned-secret methods, role-aware drills, transcript
analysis, lifecycle commands, evidence discipline, and outcome calibration.

This package reorganizes and substantially rewrites these foundations for the
Agent Skill Kit portability contract. It adds capability-aware operation across
coding agents and web/desktop hosts, bounded load-on-demand state, an
authoritative Current Truth head, atomic verified state updates, global-profile
learning, privacy and prompt-injection safeguards, and focused progressive
disclosure.
