# Material selection

The Material Library is optional. Existing brand rules, supplied references, and the user's chosen direction win. Do not replace an intentional gradient, native control, repeated component, or restrained system merely to demonstrate originality.

Use the index to shortlist two or three genuinely different material directions when exploration is useful. Compare type voice, contrast, surface treatment, control character, and imagery requirements. Let the product job determine layout separately. A bespoke direction is equally valid; the catalog is not a mandatory default or an industry-to-style lookup.

Each profile provides semantic colors, typography roles and fallbacks, surfaces, controls, focus, motion, imagery, appropriate conditions, failure modes, and two different job examples. Read only selected profiles. View the specimen at relevant widths if rendering is available. Its font stacks require no network; selected production fonts require actual availability and appropriate rights. Specimen colors pass only the declared foreground/background pairs, not every possible combination or overall accessibility.

The specimen compares the same local session preview and compact decision workspace in all twelve materials. A separate definition-list legend describes the palette and type without looking like product controls. Each direction adds only the cues needed to demonstrate its character: selection and numeric alignment, type rhythm, grouped color, or surface treatment. These are demonstrations of portability, not suggested production layouts. Do not copy their sample facts into a real product. A dark style does not imply that every app must add a theme switch. Verify every mode the actual product supports. Exercise the specimen’s empty session error, valid local preview, keyboard focus, row selection, status filters, and empty result with recovery. Its preview never creates a reservation. Status placement responds to the available panel width, so compare both stacked mobile panels and narrow columns in a wide viewport. The open-check counts are explicitly fictional and include zero; preserve meaningful values when adapting this pattern.

Keep structure, materials, and content independent. If two outcomes look interchangeable after changing their names, revisit the task structure or subject-specific information rather than adding ornament. Use the seed generator only to diversify open choices; do not randomize brand locks or force variation across a coherent product family.

Prioritize user-owned assets and actual product captures. Record source, creator, license, modifications, and intended use for third-party material. Do not present stock or generated images as documentary proof of the customer's people, place, or product. Missing assets call for an honest placeholder, a suitable no-image direction, or an authorized generation workflow. Credentials alone are not permission to generate paid comps.

Place and Light includes an original, explicitly fictional schematic and a useful text caption as its no-photo fallback. This demonstrates an honest alternative when approved location imagery is unavailable, not a finished photo-led direction. Replace it only with authorized assets or verified location information. The specimens do not validate every profile recommendation, production font, motion treatment, or assistive-technology interaction; report those limits separately.
