# AI Motion Graphics Direction Help

## What this skill does

Turn a product, footage, architectural reference, process, or sourced story into a coherent motion graphics treatment and production package. The skill develops the central visual idea, reference roles, design system, timed storyboard, motion and typography rules, prompt package, generation route, assembly instructions, and quality checks. It can direct compatible connected tools and inspect resulting media when the host supports those actions.

The skill supports six profiles and two variants within the 2D explainer profile. Profiles choose the production method; operations choose what to do with it. They can be combined in natural language without special command syntax. The planning model and generation platform are configurable. Work about service pricing, finding customers, or building a motion business is outside its scope.

## Modes

### Operations

| Operation | Use it for |
| --- | --- |
| `plan` | Develop a brief, visual mechanic, design direction, storyboard, route, and risks. |
| `create` | Prepare and, when requested and possible, generate or composite a completed motion piece. |
| `revise` | Repair, extend, adapt, localize, or restyle while preserving approved work. |
| `audit` | Inspect a prompt, design sheet, plan, storyboard, or actual clip and report findings. |
| `prompt-only` | Produce complete prompts, settings, references, assembly notes, and checks without generation. |
| `help` | Show this guide without starting production. |

### Profiles

| Profile | Use it for |
| --- | --- |
| `launch-video` | Product and feature reveals from a brief, website, or authorized motion reference. |
| `live-footage-overlay` | Speech-timed text, tracked callouts, and layered graphics over supplied footage. |
| `hyper-motion` | Product assemblies, decompositions, transformations, material studies, and camera choreography. Energy can be high or restrained. |
| `real-estate-3d` | Architectural reveals, blueprint-to-built motion, registered linework, and approved property facts. |
| `explainer-2d` | Process and product explanation in `pure-vector` or `hybrid-2d-3d` form. |
| `editorial-explainer` | Sourced narratives, mixed media, typography, diagrams, and accurate data graphics. |

`pure-vector` uses controlled illustration shapes and animation; `hybrid-2d-3d` combines flat graphic layers with rendered objects while preserving each layer's appearance. They are variants of the same profile, not additional operations.

## Start here

Provide the subject, what the audience should understand, desired format or example, duration and destination if known, and the output you want. Attach available product or brand assets, footage, approved copy, voice or music, plans, renders, or data sources. Say what each reference should control and what is already approved. Missing routine creative decisions can be handled with labeled assumptions.

You can ask for a written treatment, an HTML/SVG design sheet, a storyboard or animatic, final prompts, actual media, focused repairs, or an edit-ready handoff. For rendering, specify your preferred provider or model and any attempt or budget limit. The skill checks available tools before promising a route. Exact copy and charts may be built as controlled graphics over generated footage; a pure generation route remains available when it fits your requirements.

Installing this skill does not connect a video service. It can use a compatible native tool, plugin, MCP connection, API, browser workflow, or local renderer that your current host actually exposes. If none is connected, ask for connection guidance for your current app and chosen provider, or request a prompt-only package to run elsewhere. It does not assume Higgsfield, fal, Kie, WaveSpeed, a particular frontier model, an installed editor, or a subscription.

For existing footage, it normally preserves picture and sound while adding graphics. If you want new dialogue, voice synthesis, or invented camera angles, specify that transformation and the authorized source material. For property and editorial work, provide approved factual sources so attractive visuals do not invent facts.
