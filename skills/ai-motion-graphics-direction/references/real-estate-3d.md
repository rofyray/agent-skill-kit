# Real Estate 3D Profile

Direct an architectural reveal from approved renders, plans, elevations, floor plans, models, or a clearly fictional concept. Establish whether the deliverable is a conceptual visualization or a representation of an actual property. A photoreal result is not evidence that invented rooms, dimensions, views, finishes, or amenities are accurate.

## Geometry and fact intake

Identify the source that owns massing, floor count, setbacks, facade rhythm, windows, circulation, apartment layout, site, and material palette. Prefer authoritative plans or models for geometry, with renders supplying appearance. If only one exterior still exists, limit movement to supported views or mark unseen geometry as a concept requiring review. Do not silently invent interiors and present them as the property.

Maintain a fact sheet for areas, units, prices, dates, views, sun orientation, and amenities. Use approved units and rounding. Omit missing claims or show explicit placeholders in planning output; do not invent urgency, sales status, pricing, or guarantees. No narrator or label should imply engineering, safety, or contractual verification from a generated visualization.

## Blueprint-to-built recipe

1. Define one shared geometric scaffold and a recognizable structural or facade anchor.
2. Plot a section or axonometric using consistent line weights, dimensions, hatching, and hierarchy.
3. Move the drawing into space with continuously traceable lines when a continuous take is intended.
4. Materialize through the actual line network: specify the trigger, floor or element order, and where concrete, glazing, timber, and planting arrive.
5. Follow a feasible camera route to the important space. An interior reveal needs geometry support. A passage through glass is an intentional visualization device, not physical camera access.
6. Register proof linework to its material twin and resolve into the agreed final composition.

If the look requires geometry-bound materialization, distinguish it from a screen wipe. The transition should originate at an element and propagate through adjacent structure; avoid an arbitrary boundary sliding across the frame. After materialization, keep completed geometry present. A later plan view or final un-plot is an explicitly timed exception, not an unexplained reversion.

## Camera, light, and graphics

Map position, orientation, altitude, travel speed, and continuity at each seam. Check whether an orbit, roof descent, interior glide, terrace exit, and wide pullback can fit the duration without unreadable travel. Reduce the journey or lengthen the treatment when needed. Preserve lens behavior unless a lens change is intentionally designed.

Use directional daylight, stable reflections, believable texture scale, and a continuous lighting transition if moving toward sunset. Lock the design color target while allowing physical illumination to affect surfaces; exact graphic swatches belong in the overlay. Do not demand unchanging pixel values from a colored rail under radically different lighting.

Place dimensions and leader lines on stable anchors. Protect text from edge distortion and obstructions; reserve title and source-note zones. Text is controlled in the composite when factual accuracy matters. A final hold and a return to blank blueprint require separate timed intervals; do not fit both into the same last second without allocating time.

## Original prompt pattern

For an approved courtyard housing model, plot the courtyard perimeter, extrude the documented volumes in order, then pull back to reveal how the entry path connects them. Carry a single approved area label with its anchor. Match the final camera to an approved render. Keep any unsupported apartment interior out of the shot.

## Acceptance and fallback

Compare the result to geometry and fact sources: floor count, opening positions, setbacks, layout, area labels, units, orientation, and persistent structure. Check camera seams, registered linework, reflection changes, texture scale, and reading time. If generation changes geometry, use approved rendered views or actual model renders as plates and animate the linework separately. If a single take cannot be achieved, propose a shorter path or an assembled continuity treatment and label the change accurately. Never promise editable 3D geometry when the tool only produces pixels.
