# Expansion evaluation, 2026-10-29

This is the historical first expansion run. The [visual-review revision report](results-revision-2026-10-29.md) records the fuller state-based rereview, subsequent targeted changes, and later browser-enabled workflow evaluation. Counts below describe this earlier run; the maintained browser specimen function has since expanded its coverage.

The expansion adds Redesign and Audit, preservation and evidence protocols, and twelve optional material directions. It remains one skill. The tests support the new capabilities and expose their limits; they do not establish generally superior visual output.

## Deterministic validation

- Repository validator: 17 skills passed.
- Full unit suite: 164 tests, no failures, 18 skips. The skips are unrelated audit-code-slop integration cases requiring their optional test environment.
- Expansion tests: 21 passed. Coverage includes stale evidence, false completion, budget-limited results, unknown contracts, unexplained contract changes, safe initialization, catalog path containment, contrast, and specimen/profile drift.
- System skill validator: passed.
- Material validator: zero errors across the twelve profiles and declared contrast pairs. This is not a complete accessibility audit.
- Packages: all 17 archives rebuilt. The design archive contains 30 files, including all twelve profiles, specimens, credits, and helpers. Design and understanding archives passed ZIP integrity and exclusion checks. The design helpers ran successfully from a separately extracted package.

## Browser evidence

All twelve material specimens were measured at 320, 390, 720, 721, 1280, and 1920 pixels: 72 checks without document overflow. Phone and desktop captures were produced for every specimen and inspected. Twelve specimen journeys passed invalid-selection handling, keyboard submission, local confirmation, filtering, and reset.

The eight comparison briefs produced sixteen artifacts. The final browser matrix passed 82 selected assertions across both arms: phone/desktop document overflow, booking selection, menu filtering/reset, live word count/clear, invalid and valid signup, operations filtering/empty recovery, settings validation/save/reload/analytics, and museum date/party validation/local confirmation. These are selected journeys, not exhaustive product certification.

Additional browser checks covered the task-board rerun's loading, empty, denied, failed, and retry states; recovery restored two records and preserved a numeric zero. All eight route disclosures opened and closed with the expected expanded state. The representative-slice ordering was verified through source/logic logs; builders did not have a browser gate before propagation.

Two important findings were retained:

1. The expanded operations artifact initially failed Reset because a named form control shadowed the native reset method. Its builder's synthetic checks missed this. The original artifact/report were archived, the builder corrected the call, and the real browser rerun passed. This was an artifact repair, not a new universal instruction in the skill.
2. A visual critic flagged mobile owner/priority information outside the first operations screenshot. Browser inspection established that the baseline retained those columns in a horizontally scrollable table. The observation remains a discoverability concern, not a missing-data defect; no repair was made on that unsupported premise.

## Independent behavioral execution

The catalog retains fifteen existing cases and adds twenty. A forward evaluator received the twenty new natural requests and relevant artifacts without the expected-answer assertions. Twenty responses were produced; this is not a claim of twenty fully verified workflows.

The responses respected read-only review, existing brand constraints, bespoke exploration, component scope, permission for paid media, and narrow CSS scope. They kept screenshot critique separate from functional proof, rejected stale completion evidence, preserved required controls when reviewers disagreed, and reported unfinished mobile/recovery checks when the budget ended. Missing-browser and localization checks remained explicitly unverified. A source audit reproduced malformed-storage and failed-storage-write defects in the supplied settings fixture without claiming browser coverage or silently fixing pre-existing behavior.

The first dashboard audit, task-board, and eight-route requests lacked matching input artifacts. Their responses demonstrated missing-input handling only. The task-board and eight-route cases were subsequently rerun with suitable fixtures, then checked in the browser as described above. The dashboard case remains limited to missing-input handling.

The known-export-defect case initially restyled an unrelated available Mend page. This failed the intended scope boundary. A narrow target-identity instruction was added, then a fresh evaluator declined to substitute Mend for the missing export page and left the source unchanged. This development correction preceded held-out execution.

A final fresh forward pass returned Help with all eight modes and sixteen examples. Three understanding-skill routing requests selected Redesign for substantial visual work, retained a mechanical CSS fix locally, and used a read-only fallback when the specialist was unavailable. Those requests had no target application, so they verify routing and missing-input handling, not completed implementations.

## Blind rendered comparisons

Each arm used a fresh builder with the same brief and tool constraints. Builders could inspect their designated skill and relevant fixture, but not the other arm or expected outcome. They could not use the shared browser; rendering and functional checks were centralized. Four fresh critics each reviewed two anonymous pairs at 390 and 1280 pixels using the same visual rubric. They saw screenshots, not source, skill identity, rationale, or prior scores.

| Brief | Preferred arm | Main visible reason |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Repair booking | Previous | Earlier action/price and more economical mobile hierarchy |
| Cafe menu | Expanded | Clearer filters/reset and tighter mobile composition |
| Developer tool | Previous | More deliberate editor/result grouping and rhythm |
| Signup component | Previous | Stronger editorial hierarchy and responsive arrangement |
| Operations app | Expanded | Clear controls and immediately visible mobile record details |
| Brand restyle | Expanded, narrowly | Better desktop field alignment and emphasis |
| Settings restructure, held out | Previous, narrowly | More compact grouping and clearer spacing |
| Museum visit, held out | Previous | More coherent typography and economical mobile layout |

The expanded arm was preferred in three of eight comparisons; the previous arm in five. Several expanded outputs used excess vertical space or weaker hierarchy. Those losses are retained. The sample does not justify a claim that the expansion reliably improves aesthetics, and no preset or universal style ban was added to chase these judgments.

The two held-out briefs were defined in advance and withheld from execution until a source fingerprint was recorded. They were not unknown to the author. A later editorial change removed two duplicated mode-definition bullets from the return-contract section; the same definitions and return requirements remain elsewhere. No other skill file changed after the freeze.

Protocol deviations and limits: anonymous labels were counterbalanced rather than randomly assigned; each pair received one critic judgment, with no repeat-judge estimate; expanded operations was judged after its browser-discovered repair. Exact token cost and latency were not measured. No paid media generation or external asset fetch was used in the fixture builds. Cross-client execution, assistive technologies, full keyboard coverage, and every localization/zoom combination were not tested.

## Reproduction and retained evidence

The tracked case catalog, rendered briefs, fixtures, unit tests, and browser functions define repeatable inputs. `browser-specimens.js` checks the catalog; `browser-comparisons.js` checks generated comparison artifacts through an existing Playwright-compatible host. Neither is a required skill runtime dependency.

Local raw evidence is retained in ignored `output/design-expansion/`: the evaluation manifest, source fingerprints, initial failures, forward responses, four blind reviews, browser results, unit logs, and package checks. Captures and browser logs are under ignored `output/playwright/design-expansion/`. Raw artifacts are intentionally excluded from the installable ZIPs.
