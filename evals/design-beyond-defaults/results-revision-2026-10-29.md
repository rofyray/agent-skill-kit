# Visual-review revision, 2026-10-29

This revision implements the targeted improvements approved after the full visual review. It retains one skill and twelve optional material profiles. It strengthens whole-task criticism, responsive composition, honest evidence, and useful material demonstrations without imposing one preferred layout or a numerical beauty target.

The revision, forward evaluation, and packaging are complete. The behavioral checks passed, while visual preferences remained mixed. The evidence supports the more careful workflow, not a claim that it always produces better-looking interfaces.

## What changed

- Review entry, populated, invalid/recovery, and completion states together, with state and viewport tradeoffs, confidence, and ties.
- Improve mobile and intermediate composition while preserving the chosen identity. No universal above-the-fold, small-heading, or compact-spacing rule.
- Capture settled UI and distinguish native popover timing, local scrolling, field containment, and missing route-specific evidence.
- Remove the inherited default 9/10 target. Complete against task requirements and resolved material findings; keep visual preference separate from functional proof.
- Clarify when fresh critics see only one current artifact versus matched anonymous comparison views.

The specimens keep their IDs, catalog values, shared fictional tasks, local fonts, and absence of external assets. They now have separate legends, panel-responsive row hierarchy, selected rows, numeric data including zero, invalid/local confirmation states, filtering, empty recovery, and accessible control names. Technical Ledger, Color Geometry, and Place and Light demonstrate their defining ideas more clearly. Place and Light uses an explicitly original fictional schematic fallback; it does not claim to demonstrate verified photographic content.

## Interpretation limits

This is one generated artifact per arm per brief, judged by model critics. The eight familiar briefs are regressions; only the two newly authored briefs were held out from instruction development for this run. Initial and final judgments use different critics, so preference changes combine artifact changes with reviewer variability. Counterbalanced labels are a documented deviation from the protocol's preferred random assignment. No significance, general success rate, time saving, or token/cost advantage is claimed.

The evaluation covers local self-contained interfaces without production fonts, photographs, external services, or integration into a larger application. Browser automation and screenshots provide evidence for selected states and requirements, not exhaustive accessibility or production readiness. Native validation capture behavior depends on the capture path and timing; the readable settled Fieldnote captures show why a transient raster anomaly should not be generalized into a native-control defect. A revised Mend builder repaired a session-option DOM mismatch before its initial freeze; preliminary observations are retained, but there is no claim of an immutable pre-repair source snapshot.

Canonical skill instructions stayed frozen throughout the forward evaluation. Criticism of an individual artifact was corrected within the paired workflow where appropriate; visual losses alone did not trigger instruction edits or additional styling rounds to force a favorable result. The material demonstrations remain selection aids rather than mandatory page templates.
